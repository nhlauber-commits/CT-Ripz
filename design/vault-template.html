    How each screen differs

    • HomeAn engraved certificate. One large ornamented plate with the rosette as its seal, a ledger of big numerals, a stack of legendaries that slowly deals its top card to the back, the odds as a ruled table, and the last pulls in a display case with a foil lip.
    • StoreA shop shelf. Theme tiles carry a thin theme-colour edge; the three packs stand on a gold shelf strip with price-board numerals underneath. Each pack has an information button that opens its odds. A pack you cannot afford shows a muted button and how many coins short you are.
    • InventoryAn album page. Index tabs across the top, a stitched spine with ring holes, cards in recessed slots, empty slots hatched and numbered, and the sell confirmation built into the page.
    • OpeningA velvet stage under one light. Cards arrive face down with an engraved back and turn over one at a time; foil follows the pointer on Rare and better.
