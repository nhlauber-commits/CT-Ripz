# design/: design hand-offs and studies

- [`TEXTURES.md`](TEXTURES.md) is the hand-off to Nick (Higgsfield). To ask for a texture, add a
  row there: file name, size, use, prompt. Nick generates it into `public/textures/`, ticks the
  row and commits. Do not generate images yourself unless you are Nick.
- [`ripz-template.html`](ripz-template.html) is the current look ("Hyperprint"), as a static review
  page. `vault-template.html` is the earlier navy and gold study, kept for history.
- Static design pages (`sheet.html`, `body-font.html`) and the mood board (`moodboard/`) go here
  when a task asks for them. They are for review, not shipped in the app.
- The art direction they follow is [`docs/brief.md`](../docs/brief.md).
