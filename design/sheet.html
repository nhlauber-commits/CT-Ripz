6 Logo

7 How each screen uses it

Home

The one ornamented screen. A wide hero plate with the large logo, the Inline title set left, the rosette centred behind it and corner ornaments. Stats sit under it as a price board, not as three boxes.

Store

A shop wall. Theme tiles become a row of drawer labels with a thin theme-colour edge. Each pack tier stands on a shelf with a foil front edge and a board-style price. No framed plates here.

Inventory

An album. One page per theme with a stitched spine on the left, ruled card slots, and dashed outlines for missing cards. Filters read as index tabs along the top of the page. The rosette is faint.

Opening overlay

A display case. Dark navy cloth, hatching at the edges, a single gold rule around the stage and no rosette, so the cards are the only detail. Foil follows the pointer on the revealed card.
