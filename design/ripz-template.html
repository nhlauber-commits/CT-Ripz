    How each screen differs

    • HomeA gig poster on a violet sunburst. Hot-pink hero with the title printed out of register, three ledger tiles in yellow, cyan and lime, a fanned stack of legendaries, odds drawn as rarity-coloured bars, and the last pulls tossed onto a checkered counter.
    • StoreThe backdrop turns teal. Theme tiles are split in each theme's two colours; the chosen one tips over and gets a yellow keyline. Packs are crimped foil wrappers standing in a sunburst of the theme's colours, with slanted price stickers on a perforated ticket strip.
    • InventoryThe backdrop turns magenta. A dot-grid album page with a hazard-stripe spine, theme-coloured index tabs, dashed card slots, halftone empty slots and pink duplicate counters. The sell confirmation is built into a yellow strip.
    • OpeningA fast pink and violet sunburst. Cards land face down and crooked on a yellow and cyan checker back, straighten and flip with a bounce; holo foil follows the pointer.
